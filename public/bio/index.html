Bio

Hi! I am Daisuke. I am a predoctoral researcher and a PhD candidate (sowi:docs Fellowship) at the Department of Communication, University of Vienna, Austria. I am affiliated with both Computational Communication Science Lab (CCL) and Vienna Media Change and Innovation (VMCI) Lab, supervised by Univ.-Prof. Dr. Annie Waldherr and Univ.-Prof. Dr. Desirée Schmuck. I was previously working as a student research assistant to Annie Waldherr at CCL.

In my dissertation project, I focus on the types, prevalence, audience engagement, and effects of edits (a particular genre of short-form video) about politicians on TikTok and YouTube Shorts. I believe this is a particularly interesting form of content because those edits can provide political content in a playful way, but they can also influence/bias/distort our perception of politicians without engaging in critical thinking. Here, content creators can be real humans, but can also be bots, who knows!

More broadly, I am generally interested in political communication on social media. I like to use platform observational data and computational methods in social science to understand what is going on on those platforms, but I (am eager to) use experiments, simulations, qualitative content analysis, etc, depending on the research questions.
